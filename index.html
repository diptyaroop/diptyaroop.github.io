Diptyaroop Maji

AI systems · Distributed systems · Applied ML · Sustainable computing

I am a PhD candidate in the College of Information and Computer Sciences at UMass Amherst, advised by Prof. Ramesh K. Sitaraman. I also work closely with Prof. Prashant Shenoy.

My research spans AI systems and distributed computing, with a focus on sustainability. I develop machine learning methods to forecast the carbon footprint of electricity grids and real-world systems, and design systems that use these signals to guide operational decisions.

I am also currently a Student Researcher at Google's Systems Research Group , where I am working on scheduling ML workloads across geo-distributed clusters. Previously, I worked at VMware Research (internship), IIT Bombay, and Samsung Research Institute Bangalore.

Expected PhD graduation: May 2027.

Research

I work on both systems for machine learning and machine learning for systems. My current research directions include:

  • Systems for AI. Scheduling ML workloads across geographically distributed clusters to reduce emissions while maintaining resource occupancy and meeting user constraints.
  • Machine learning for systems. Using time series foundation models and forecasting methods to support infrastructure decisions, including CarbonX and CarbonCast.
  • Sustainable distributed infrastructure. Designing carbon-aware application delivery and studying how carbon accounting and renewable energy availability affect system optimization.

My broader systems background includes high-performance 5G dataplanes and ongoing work on neural loss recovery for volumetric videoconferencing.
